What Xomify does, and how

Xomify sits on top of your Spotify account and keeps the history Spotify doesn't: what you played, month by month, and what your friends thought of the songs you sent them.

Your data

  • Xomify authenticates through Spotify's OAuth flow. It never receives your password.
  • It reads your listening history, top items and saved tracks, and creates playlists on your behalf. It does not modify playlists it didn't create.
  • Shares, ratings and comments are visible to the friends you share with — nothing is public.
  • Push notifications are per-device and per-type. Every kind can be turned off individually in the iOS app, and muting one still keeps its entry in your in-app notifications.
