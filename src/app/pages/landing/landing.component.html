Your Spotify year, all year.

Xomify watches what you actually listen to, turns it into playlists you didn't have to make, and lets you push a song at a friend and find out what they thought of it.

Music Taste

The songs, artists and genres you actually play

Not a once-a-year summary. Your top tracks, artists and genres, ranked across every window from the last four weeks to all time — updated as your listening changes, with the ranks you had last month still there to compare against.

  • Songs, artists and genres, each with their own leaderboard
  • Every liked song, searchable
  • Jump from any artist straight into their albums and singles
Monthly Wrapped

A Wrapped playlist every month, built without you

On the first of the month Xomify snapshots what you played and drops a playlist straight into your Spotify. The month after that, it does it again — and keeps every one, so you can go back to what a particular March sounded like.

  • Generated automatically, no button to remember to press
  • Full history, month by month
  • Email and push when a new one lands
Release Radar

Every new release from artists you follow

Once a week Xomify checks everyone you follow on Spotify and collects what actually came out — albums, singles, the lot — as a calendar and a playlist. Nothing from an algorithm guessing what you might like. Just the artists you already chose.

  • Calendar and list views of the week's releases
  • Queue anything straight from the page
  • Full history of previous weeks
Playlist Builder

Build a playlist, then find out what's in it

Drag tracks into a queue from anywhere in the app and push the result to Spotify. Then run it through analysis and see what you actually made — the tempo, the mood, how much of it is the same three artists.

  • Drag-and-drop queue that follows you across pages
  • Playlist analysis: tempo, energy, artist concentration
  • Mood picks when you know the feeling but not the songs
Shares

Send someone a song and actually hear back

Sending a track in a group chat means never finding out whether anyone played it. Share it through Xomify and you get the other half: who queued it, who played it, and what they rated it out of five.

  • Queue it into their Spotify in one tap
  • Star ratings, reactions and comments on every share
  • Notified the moment someone listens or rates
Discovery

The rest of it

Mood-based recommendations, concerts near you from artists in your rotation, and music news — the smaller surfaces that make the app worth opening when you're not looking for anything in particular.
